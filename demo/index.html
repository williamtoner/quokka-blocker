Town council votes to keep the bandstand, for now

By A. Reporter · 6 min read

After three hours of debate the council agreed to defer a decision on the future of the Victorian bandstand until the spring, when a survey of its ironwork is due. Residents filled the public gallery.

"It is the one place in town where everyone has a memory," said one. Others were less sentimental about the cost of repairs, which officers estimate could run to six figures.

Advertisement

The bandstand was built in 1891 with money raised by public subscription. It has hosted brass bands, weddings, a short-lived jazz festival and, for one memorable summer, a production of Hamlet.

Councillors will receive the survey in March. Until then, the bandstand remains open, and the brass band will play as usual on Sunday afternoons.
