1. In Chrome, open https://williamtoner.net/quokka-blocker/store/ and click "Download the store kit". When the download finishes, open the Downloads folder in Finder and double-click quokka-blocker-store-kit.zip, so it unpacks into a folder called quokka-blocker-store-kit in Downloads. Do not unpack the quokka-blocker-chrome.zip inside that folder: it is uploaded as it is. Keep the store kit page open in its own tab for the rest of the task.
