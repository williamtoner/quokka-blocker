Quokka Blocker swaps the adverts on the web for photographs and short films of quokkas, otters, red pandas and their friends: fitted to every space, faces never cropped, quietly lovely.

This is the real extension's renderer and photo library, running in your browser. Hover an animal.

◳

Fits every space

Every picture is marked with where its animal is, so a crop never loses a face. Long banners become filmstrips; tall towers stack up.

◐

Swaps before you see

Ad spaces are spotted as the page builds, usually before the advert inside has loaded. No flashes, no jumping text, no gaps.

❖

Your animals

Choose who you meet, from capybaras to snow monkeys, or go all in with Quokkas only. Short silent films appear now and then; switch them off any time.

◎

Nothing leaves your browser

No accounts, no tracking, no servers. Every photo ships inside the extension. Pause it on any site, or bring back any ad with a click.

Hundreds of pictures. Dozens of animals.

Chosen one by one from the work of wildlife photographers who share their pictures openly. Every one is credited, on hover and in full.

Install Quokka Blocker

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc

  1. Download for Chrome and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.

Firefox

  1. Download for Firefox.
  2. Open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox.
  3. Click Load Temporary Add-on… and choose the zip.

Until Mozilla signs it, Firefox forgets temporary add-ons when it restarts.
