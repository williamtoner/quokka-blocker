Quokka Blocker is on

Every ad, now an animal.

From here on, the adverts you come across become photographs and short films of quokkas, otters, red pandas and their friends: laid out to fit each space exactly, so pages look as they should, only kinder.

01

It finds the ad spaces

As a page loads, Quokka Blocker spots the spaces the big ad networks fill, usually before the advert inside has even appeared.

02

Animals move in

Each space gets an animal picked to suit its shape: one picture for a box, a little filmstrip for a long banner. Faces are never cut off.

03

Hover for more

See who is who and who took the picture, ask for Another, or choose Show ad to put the original back.

Pin Quokka Blocker to your toolbar to see how many ads have become animals, pause it on a site, switch films off, or choose your animals. In Chrome, open the puzzle-piece menu and press the pin.

Nothing you browse leaves your computer. No accounts, no tracking, no network requests of its own. Photo credits
